# Recipe Style Guide

Conventions for writing recipes in this collection. Read this before adding or editing a recipe.

## Recipe

- Maximum of 5 steps from start to finish.
- Casual tone of voice.
- Encourage using smell, sight, and touch.
- One or two intro lines under the hero image, before the ingredients.

## Peanut Gallery

The comments under each recipe. This is the fun part, and it should sound like us.

### Format

```markdown
## Peanut Gallery

**Carolyn:** A comment is a name in bold, a colon, and whatever you want to say.

**Tim:** Replies are just the next comment.

A comment can keep going in a new paragraph, and can include a photo:

![Tim's attempt](../images/larb/tims-attempt.jpg)

**Nate:** Everything up to the next bold name belongs to the comment above it.
```

Any name works. The five siblings have set colors; anyone else gets one automatically.

### Real comments

- **The recipe's author does not comment on their own recipe.**
- Use people's actual words. Paste from the group chat and fix typos only if they'd be embarrassed. Don't polish.
- Any shape is fine: a one-liner, a reply, an argument, a story. There's no required length and it doesn't have to be a conversation.
- Aim for at least one memory or inside joke per recipe. Tips are welcome but optional.
- Snark is encouraged. Keep it the kind Mom would laugh at.

### Filler comments

Until the real comments are written, a recipe has placeholder comments and `gallery: "filler"` in its frontmatter. The build lists every recipe still marked as filler, and the page shows a small "placeholder" note.

**Remove the `gallery` line once every comment on the page was written by the person it's attributed to.**

Rules for writing filler:

- 2 to 4 people, drawn from Nate, Alex, Ben, Tim, and Carolyn. Never the author.
- Snarky and funny. Vary the shape from recipe to recipe: not every gallery is a skeptic, an explainer, and a closing line.
- Include at least one suggestion that genuinely makes the dish better.
- Don't invent family memories, or anything about Mom. Those have to come from the real people.
